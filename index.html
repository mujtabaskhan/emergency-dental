A sudden toothache at 2 a.m., a cracked crown on Eid morning, a child's broken front tooth after a football match—dental disasters never check the clock. That's why Alina Dental Clinic keeps its lights on, and its phones answered long after other practices have locked up for the night.

With branches in DHA and Wapda Town, we deliver rapid, expert care for every dental emergency in Lahore, restoring comfort and saving smiles when every minute counts.

Pain can escalate quickly: an untreated abscess may spread the infection to the jaw or even the bloodstream; a knocked-out tooth loses viability with each passing minute. Acting fast often means distinguishing between a simple procedure and a complex surgery. Our clinic has experienced dentists, digital X-ray imaging, and a fully stocked surgical suite, so you don't have to wait.

Common Emergencies We Treat (at a Glance)

  • Severe toothache or abscess
  • Knocked-out (avulsed) tooth
  • Broken, chipped, or cracked teeth
  • Lost fillings, crowns, or bridges
  • Gum or cheek lacerations
  • Orthodontic wire pokes or loose brackets
  • Facial swelling or dental trauma after accidents

We encourage patients to call the helpline 03111248888 the moment trouble strikes. Our triage nurse will advise first-aid steps—preserving a tooth in milk, managing bleeding, or controlling swelling—while arranging an immediate chairside slot at the nearest branch.
